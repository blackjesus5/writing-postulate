# Sources and decisions

This repository adapts methods. It does not install or reproduce upstream
skills.

## Writing methods

| Source | Kept | Refused |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Beautiful Prose | Concrete nouns, varied cadence, substantive openings, no filler or decorative triplets | Coldness as a default, a universal ban on headings, imitation of a named register |
| Simple English | Mechanism first, defined terms, condition before command, one action per procedure step | Global word caps and a maintenance-manual voice for letters or essays |
| Humanizer | Detection of filler, vague claims, inflated significance, repetitive syntax | “Human” as detector evasion, rewriting that changes meaning |
| Humanize AI Text | A review pass for chatbot artifacts, promotional language, and forced contrasts | AI probability scores and bypass claims |
| Summarize | Reader-purpose and adjustable-depth summaries | Compression that removes uncertainty or decision context |
| Documentation expert | Question, authoritative source, version, and example before drafting | Product-specific assumptions outside its documentation set |

## Document formats

Markdown conversion, DOCX, PDF, and PPTX methods contribute one principle:
format carries meaning. Preserve structure and validate the rendered result.
They do not determine the prose voice.

## Marketing and DevRel

Customer research, product marketing, content strategy, copywriting, marketing
psychology, AI SEO, CRO, ads, launch, and analytics contribute a shared
sequence: source language, product truth, proof, channel adaptation, then
measurement.

The skill refuses:

- Claims not supported by shipped artifacts or sources.
- Customer quotations invented for a page.
- Manufactured urgency, scarcity, consensus, fear, or social proof.
- Thin search pages and keyword padding.
- A funnel frame applied to a technical, compassionate, or private document.

## Podcast influence

A private two-speaker analysis informs general craft principles only:

- Orient before depth.
- Surface construct, then core feature.
- Concrete instance before abstraction.
- Steady, non-evaluative warmth.
- Personal journey as owned evidence, not diagnosis.
- Repair a jump by restating at a deeper level.
- Leave room for the other person's knowledge.
- Short settle after a layered thought.

The recording, transcript, speaker names, and private analysis stay out of this
repository. The skill does not imitate either speaker.
