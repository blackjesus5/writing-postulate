# Guide

The postulate has two layers.

The invariant layer does not change: concrete facts, evidence distinct from
interpretation, an explicit limit, privacy, and no formulaic AI prose.

The register changes because readers need different things.

## Choose a register

| If the task is | Use | The reader needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A decision from a long source | Summary | Claim, evidence, uncertainty, decision, next action |
| A README, spec, or system explanation | Technical | Mechanism, terms, stages, exact limits |
| A step someone must follow | Procedure | Condition, action, risk |
| A class, guide, or onboarding page | Teaching | Orientation, familiar example, then mechanism |
| A public reflection or essay | Sophisticated prose | Careful argument, cadence, image where it clarifies |
| A sensitive explanation | Compassionate | Steady context, dignity, no therapy script |
| A message to one person | Letter | Directness, warmth, a plain request, room for reply |
| A claim about a method or record | Evidence brief | Claim, source, status, boundary |
| A launch, landing page, or DevRel asset | Marketing | Audience language, product proof, one action |

## Route in order

1. **Operation.** Is the task to summarize, explain, rewrite, create, or
   market?
2. **Artifact.** Is it a README, procedure, lesson, letter, office file, post,
   or evidence brief?
3. **Audience.** What decision, understanding, or action does this person need?
4. **Boundary.** Is the source public, protected, or local only?
5. **Register.** Use the table above.

Do not use the most lyrical register just because the subject is human. Do not
use the most compressed register just because the source is technical.

## What changes, and what stays

| Changes by purpose | Stays stable |
| --- | --- |
| Sentence length and cadence | Facts and evidence |
| Paragraph shape | Uncertainty |
| Degree of directness | Privacy boundary |
| Use of tables, steps, or prose | Source terms and quotations |
| Warmth and first person | No diagnosis or inflated claim |
| Call to action in a marketing asset | No manufactured urgency |
